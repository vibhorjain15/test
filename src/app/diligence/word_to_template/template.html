Get started with quick instructions:

  • Right click on the text to mark items as categories, sub-categories, questions, and tables etc.
  • Once you have marked categories and questions, use the 'Auto Identify Answers' button to quickly mark answers between questions.
  • To make answer selection changes after auto identifying, left click and drag the mouse to highlight the text. Right click and under 'Selected text actions' choose to 'Mark as Answer' or 'Remove from Answers'. Note - The selected answer text can be updated from marked and unmarked only after answers have been auto identified.
  • You can edit the content of answers once you navigate to the preview.
  • Use the 'Reset File' button to clear all marked items in the current file.
  • Auto identify answers will not work for grids. Please mark them manually.
