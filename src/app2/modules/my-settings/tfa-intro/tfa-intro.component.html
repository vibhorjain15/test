About Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your account. Check off one of your cybersecurity criterias by activiting this feature and help us secure your data.

STEP 1: When you sign into your DiligenceVault account, you'll enter username and password, like always. (First Level of Defense)
STEP 2: In addition, you'll need to enter an additional code that DiligenceVault sends to your phone or tablet app. Once you enter the code, you'll be logged into your DiligenceVault account. (Second Level of Defense)

Setup to get two-factor authentication codes is easy! Get started by installing Google Authenticator on your phone.
